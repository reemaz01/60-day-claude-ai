# 📈 Marketing Strategy Simulator

An interactive HTML application that teaches users how to think like a marketing strategist by building a complete marketing strategy through hands-on decision making. Instead of generating marketing plans automatically, the simulator guides users through audience analysis, platform selection, content planning, campaign execution, and real-world marketing challenges.

---

## 📌 Overview

The Marketing Strategy Simulator is an educational web application designed to help users understand the strategic thinking behind successful marketing campaigns.

Users create a business or personal brand, identify their audience, choose the right marketing channels, develop content pillars, respond to unexpected marketing situations, and receive a final Growth Report evaluating their strategic decisions.

---

## ✨ Features

- Interactive multi-step marketing simulation
- Business, Personal Brand, and Random Client modes
- Audience analysis and positioning
- Marketing platform recommendations
- Content pillar planning
- 30-Day marketing roadmap
- Marketing crisis and challenge simulations
- Dynamic Growth Score
- Personalized Growth Report
- Claude AI prompt suggestions
- Fully responsive interface
