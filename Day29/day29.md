# Operation Lifeline – Supply Chain Crisis Simulator

An interactive executive decision-making simulator that challenges players to navigate high-pressure supply chain disruptions. Take the role of a CEO, respond to crises, negotiate with suppliers, lead executive decisions, invest in AI capabilities, and measure your organization's resilience through a comprehensive executive dashboard.

---

## 📖 Overview

Operation Lifeline is a browser-based business simulation designed to teach supply chain resilience and strategic decision-making. Every playthrough generates a unique company profile and crisis scenario, requiring players to balance operational efficiency, financial performance, and customer satisfaction while managing a rapidly evolving emergency.

---

## Features

- 🏭 Randomly generated company profiles
- 🔥 Dynamic supply chain crisis scenarios
- 📊 Executive crisis response dashboard
- 🤝 Four-round supplier negotiation simulator
- 👔 CEO leadership decision scenarios
- 🤖 AI investment strategy planning
- 📈 Live KPI and operational impact tracking
- 📋 Executive performance dashboard
- 🎯 Final crisis score and performance grading
- 🌙 Fully responsive modern dark UI

# 🎮 Simulation Workflow

## 1. Company Profile

Begin by reviewing a randomly generated organization including:

- Industry
- Annual Revenue
- Manufacturing Facilities
- Warehouses
- Supplier Network
- Inventory Levels
- Global Operations

---

## 2. Crisis Detection

Analyze a major operational disruption such as:

- Factory Fire
- Supplier Failure
- Port Closure
- Transportation Strike
- Natural Disaster
- Geopolitical Conflict

Each scenario includes:

- Situation Brief
- Financial Exposure
- Business Impact
- Risk Assessment
- Decision Timeline

---

## 3. Crisis Response Dashboard

Choose operational strategies to minimize disruption.

Examples include:

- Cross-Dock Network
- Nearshore Production
- Inventory Redistribution
- Customer Communication
- Alternative Suppliers

Every decision influences:

- 💰 Cost
- 📦 Inventory
- 📈 Profit
- 🚚 Delivery Speed
- ⭐ Customer Satisfaction

---

## 4. Supplier Negotiation

Participate in a four-round negotiation where each response impacts:

- Supplier Trust
- Pricing
- Lead Time

Players must balance urgency with long-term supplier relationships.

---

## 5. CEO Boardroom

Handle executive-level leadership scenarios involving:

- Budget Allocation
- Crisis Leadership
- Shareholder Expectations
- Employee Management
- Strategic Trade-offs

---

## 6. AI Investment Strategy

Select AI technologies to strengthen future resilience.

Available investments include:

- Demand Forecasting AI
- Inventory Optimization
- Supplier Risk Monitoring
- Warehouse Vision AI

Each investment improves different operational KPIs.

# 📚 Key Learnings

### Executive Decision Making

Strategic leadership requires balancing immediate crisis response with long-term organizational resilience.

### Supply Chain Risk Management

Diversified suppliers, flexible logistics, and adequate inventory buffers reduce operational vulnerability.

### Negotiation Strategy

Successful negotiations depend on trust, timing, communication, and relationship management—not just pricing.

### Data-Driven Leadership

Monitoring operational KPIs enables faster, more informed executive decisions during uncertainty.

### AI for Supply Chain Resilience

AI technologies improve forecasting, inventory optimization, supplier monitoring, and operational visibility to reduce future disruptions.

### Customer-Centric Crisis Management

Transparent communication and proactive planning help maintain customer trust even during severe operational challenges.
