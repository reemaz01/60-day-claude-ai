Nutrition Sources & Database
Transparency on where our data comes from and what's in the food database.
📚 Data Sources
🏥
ICMR-NIN Nutrient Requirements
Indian Council of Medical Research — Nutrient Requirements & RDA for Indians (2020 revision). Primary source for micronutrient targets.
🌍
WHO/FAO Dietary Reference Values
Global reference for macronutrient distribution, protein requirements per kg bodyweight, and population-level energy needs.
🇺🇸
USDA FoodData Central
Composition data for common foods. Values per 100g raw weight unless otherwise noted. Nutrients may vary by cooking method.
📐
Mifflin-St Jeor Equation (BMR)
Basal Metabolic Rate formula validated across diverse populations. Multiplied by activity factor (PAL) to get TDEE.
🔬 Calculation Methods
BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor)
Male: (10×W) + (6.25×H) − (5×A) + 5
Female: (10×W) + (6.25×H) − (5×A) − 161
Protein Target
0.8–1.2g per kg body weight (maintenance), up to 1.6g for muscle gain. Higher end for active individuals.
Macro Distribution
Carbs: 45–65% energy · Fat: 20–35% energy · Protein: 15–25% energy (AMDR)
Micronutrient Targets
Age- and gender-adjusted RDA from ICMR-NIN 2020. Iron requirements higher for menstruating females.

⚠️ Important Limitations
🔥
Cooking method changes nutrients
Boiling, frying, and roasting change calorie density and nutrient availability significantly. All database values are for raw/uncooked weight.
🌱
Soil and variety variation
Actual nutrient content varies by crop variety, soil quality, season, and region. Values shown are average estimates.
💊
Bioavailability not accounted for
The iron in plant foods (non-haem) is absorbed 2–5× less efficiently than haem iron from meat. Vitamin D from sun exposure is not tracked. Consult a dietitian for clinical accuracy.
