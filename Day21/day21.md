# Digital Footprint Dashboard

## Overview

Digital Footprint Dashboard is an interactive privacy analysis tool that transforms a user's reported digital services into a visual privacy and exposure assessment.

The dashboard helps users understand how their online activities across social media, messaging, shopping, payments, gaming, and content platforms may contribute to data collection, behavioral profiling, and privacy risks.

The report is presented as a modern single-page dashboard with interactive visualizations, risk indicators, privacy scores, and actionable recommendations.


## Features

### Privacy & Exposure Scoring

* Digital Footprint Score (0–100)
* Privacy Score (0–100)
* Exposure severity classification
* Ecosystem concentration analysis

### Exposure Heatmap

Visual representation of exposure across:

* Behavioral browsing
* Location tracking
* Social graph data
* Purchase intent
* Content preferences
* Device fingerprinting
* Financial activity
* Cross-platform identity tracking

### Company Exposure Ranking

Ranks major companies by estimated data collection breadth and profiling capability.

Examples:

* Google
* Meta
* ByteDance
* Amazon
* Spotify
* Discord

### Data Collection Matrix

Service-by-service comparison showing potential collection of:

* Location data
* Behavioral data
* Contact information
* Biometric information
* Financial information
* Content activity

### Risk Radar

Highlights major privacy threats such as:

* Cross-platform profiling
* Behavioral advertising
* Location history accumulation
* Financial behavior inference
* Biometric exposure

### Digital Twin Profile

Generates estimated insights about what data aggregators may infer from combined app usage patterns.

Examples:

* Lifestyle indicators
* Shopping behavior
* Entertainment preferences
* Device usage patterns
* Payment behavior

### Privacy Improvement Simulator

Interactive controls that allow users to test privacy actions and observe estimated score improvements.

Examples:

* Disable ad personalization
* Use a VPN
* Switch to privacy-focused messaging
* Review app permissions

### Privacy Action Plan

Provides prioritized recommendations to improve digital privacy and reduce tracking exposure.

## How It Works

1. User provides a list of digital services they use.
2. The dashboard maps services to publicly known data collection practices.
3. Exposure levels are estimated across multiple categories.
4. Scores, rankings, and visualizations are generated.
5. Privacy recommendations are displayed based on identified risks.

Important:

* No private databases are accessed.
* No personal data is collected.
* All risk assessments are estimates based on publicly documented platform behaviors.

## Disclaimer

This dashboard provides educational and informational insights only.

All exposure levels, risk scores, and inferred characteristics are estimates derived from publicly available information about platform data practices and user-reported service usage.

No private user data, databases, or proprietary tracking information is accessed.
