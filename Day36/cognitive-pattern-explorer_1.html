Welcome to the Cognitive Pattern Explorer

A calm, exploratory space to notice patterns in how you think, feel, and decide. This is not a test and it will not diagnose you — it's simply a mirror for reflection, built around five common thinking tendencies: Analytical, Emotional Intuitive, Overthinking Loop, Action-First, and Balanced Reflective.

Choose an ambience, then begin whenever you're ready.

Chapter 1 · Discover Your Thinking Style

Read each small scenario and choose the response that feels most natural to you. There are no right answers.

Chapter 2 · Choose Your Priorities

Drag each card into the column that best fits how much it matters to you right now. On touch devices, press and hold, then drag — or use the keyboard: focus a card and press Enter, then arrow keys to move it, Enter again to drop.

Chapter 3 · Map Your Thinking

Arrange these thoughts along the timeline in the order you'd typically experience them when facing a decision. Drag chips onto the track, or use keyboard: focus a chip, press Enter to pick up, arrows to move, Enter to place.
