Compare both outputs and identify:
1. Which roadmap feels more personalized?
The Full-Stack roadmap feels more personalized because it aligns directly with my goal of landing an internship. It focuses on building real-world projects, deployment, authentication, and portfolio development—skills that recruiters actively look for.

2. Which roadmap would you actually follow?
I would follow the Full-Stack roadmap as my primary path while using parts of the Python roadmap to strengthen fundamentals. The combination gives me both practical development experience and stronger programming skills.

3. What role did context play in improving the result?
Context made a huge difference. Once my current situation (4th-year student), existing skill level (Python), goal (internship), and timeline (6 months) were provided, the recommendation shifted from a generic learning plan to a roadmap tailored to my career objectives. The added context helped prioritize the skills and projects with the highest impact for my specific goal.
