Job Search & Personal Branding Toolkit

A comprehensive toolkit designed to help students, fresh graduates, and early-career professionals streamline their job search process and build a strong professional brand.

this toolkit provides ready-to-use templates, outreach messages, interview preparation resources, and career guidance specifically tailored for aspiring ML/AI and Software Development candidates.


Finding internships and entry-level opportunities can be overwhelming. This toolkit consolidates essential job search resources into one place, helping candidates communicate professionally and present themselves effectively.

The content is ATS-friendly, practical, and immediately usable.

Features

* ATS-Friendly Cover Letter Template
* Recruiter Outreach Email Template
* Hiring Manager Email Template
* LinkedIn Connection Request Message
* Referral Request Message
* Professional Follow-Up Email
* 30-Second Professional Introduction Script
* Recommended Job Titles
* Recruiter-Focused Strength Analysis
* Skill Gap Assessment
* Learning Roadmap
* Personal Branding Statements
* Interview Talking Points and STAR Stories


Toolkit Contents

 1. ATS-Friendly Cover Letter

A customizable cover letter designed for internship and fresher applications.

2. Recruiter Outreach Email

Professional cold outreach templates for recruiters.

3. Hiring Manager Email

Targeted email templates for specific teams and hiring managers.

4. LinkedIn Networking Messages

Short connection requests optimized for networking.

5. Referral Request Templates

Polite and professional referral messages.

6. Follow-Up Emails

Templates for re-engaging recruiters after applications.

7. Professional Introduction Script

A concise self-introduction suitable for interviews and networking events.

8. Career Guidance

Includes:

* Recommended job roles
* Key strengths analysis
* Skill gap identification
* Suggested learning roadmap

9. Interview Preparation

STAR-format stories, common recruiter questions, and discussion points.

1. Download or clone this repository.

4. Use the templates during your internship or job search process.
