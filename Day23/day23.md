# AI Career Mentor & Consultant – Customer & MVP Blueprint

AI Career Mentor & Consultant is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed for Indian students aged 13–25. The project explores how artificial intelligence can provide affordable, personalized career counseling, skill-gap analysis, learning roadmaps, and career planning support.

This blueprint was created to validate the business opportunity, identify target customers, and define a minimum viable product (MVP) before investing in full-scale development.



## Problem Statement

Millions of students struggle to make informed career decisions due to:

* Expensive career counseling services
* Limited access in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities
* Generic advice from search engines and social media
* Lack of structured skill-development roadmaps
* Placement and internship uncertainty

The goal is to make quality career guidance accessible, affordable, and available 24/7 through AI.



## Customer Research Findings

### Primary Customer Segment

**Students (Age 15–22)**

Characteristics:

* Android-first users
* Career-confused students
* Preparing for college or placements
* Located across Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 cities

Common Challenges:

* Unsure which career path suits them
* Don't know which skills to learn
* Fear making the wrong career decision
* Limited access to professional counseling

### Secondary Customer Segment

**Schools & Colleges**

Needs:

* Career guidance solutions for students
* Affordable counseling alternatives
* Student progress tracking
* NEP 2020 alignment



## Key Customer Insights

### Top Pain Points

1. Difficulty choosing a suitable career path
2. High counseling costs
3. Lack of local guidance resources
4. Unclear skill-development direction
5. Placement anxiety
6. Parent pressure regarding career choices
7. One-time counseling without follow-up
8. Internship discovery challenges
9. No accountability system
10. Expensive interview preparation

### Validation Insight

The problem is significant and recurring, but willingness to pay remains the biggest unknown and requires validation before product development.

## MVP Recommendation

### Build First

✅ Career assessment form

✅ AI-generated career reports

✅ Skill-gap analysis

✅ Personalized learning roadmap

✅ Mobile-friendly web application

✅ PDF report generation

### Avoid in Version 1

❌ Native mobile apps

❌ Full psychometric testing

❌ Internship marketplace

❌ Live counselors

❌ Community platform



## Validation Strategy

### Phase 1 – Customer Discovery

* Conduct 20 student interviews
* Interview counselors and educators
* Document recurring problems

### Phase 2 – Demand Validation

Create a landing page with:

* Product explanation
* Sample career report
* ₹99 pre-order offer

Success Criteria:

* 200+ visitors
* 15%+ conversion rate

### Phase 3 – Manual MVP

* Generate reports manually using AI
* Deliver to first users
* Collect NPS and feedback



## Business Model

## Key Learnings

### What Worked

* Strong and widespread customer pain
* Large underserved market
* AI significantly reduces delivery costs
* Clear value proposition for students and parents

### What Needs Validation

* Willingness to pay
* Long-term user retention
* Trust in AI-generated recommendations
* Conversion from free to paid plans

### Major Risks

1. Incorrect AI recommendations
2. Low willingness to pay
3. Competitive pressure
4. Founder burnout
5. Student data privacy compliance


## Success Metrics

* Landing page conversion > 15%
* NPS score ≥ 40
* 10+ paying users in Month 1
* Organic referrals from early adopters
* Report delivery within 48 hours



## Final Takeaway

This project demonstrates a lean startup validation approach for an AI-powered career guidance platform. Rather than building a complete product immediately, the focus is on customer discovery, demand validation, and rapid MVP testing to ensure that real users are willing to pay before significant development effort is invested.
