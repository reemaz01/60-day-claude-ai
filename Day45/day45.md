# Decision Strategist

An AI-powered **Decision Strategist** that transforms difficult choices into structured, evidence-based decisions. Instead of relying on intuition alone, it guides users through analyzing options, challenging assumptions, evaluating trade-offs, and producing a clear recommendation backed by a decision matrix.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Decision Strategist** helps users make confident decisions by combining logical frameworks with AI-generated insights. It walks through every stage of decision-making—from defining the real problem to delivering a final verdict and shareable summaries.

This project demonstrates how AI can simplify complex personal and financial decisions through structured reasoning.
