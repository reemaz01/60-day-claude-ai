# 🚚 Supply Chain Builder

An interactive educational simulation that teaches the fundamentals of supply chain management through real-world business decision-making. Players manage a randomly generated company, make strategic operational decisions, observe live KPI changes, and receive a comprehensive performance dashboard with actionable recommendations.

---

## 📌 Overview

Supply Chain Builder is a browser-based simulation designed to help users understand how strategic supply chain decisions affect business performance.

Instead of reading theory, users experience the consequences of their choices by building a supply chain for a randomly generated company. Every decision creates measurable trade-offs between cost, speed, resilience, customer satisfaction, and sustainability.

The application is built as a single-page React application running entirely in the browser without requiring a backend or external database.

---

# ✨ Features

### 🏢 Random Company Generator

Each simulation creates a unique company with:

* Company name
* Industry
* Product category
* Target markets
* Demand pattern

This makes every playthrough different and encourages experimentation.

---

### 📚 Interactive Learning

Every business decision includes:

* Plain-language explanations
* Business context
* Why the decision matters
* Real-world trade-offs

The simulator is designed for beginners while remaining useful for business students and professionals.

---

### ⚙️ Five Strategic Decision Modules

Users design their supply chain by selecting strategies for:

* Supplier Strategy
* Factory Location
* Warehouse Strategy
* Transportation Method
* Inventory Management

Each option provides different advantages and disadvantages.

---

### 📈 Live Performance Dashboard

Business metrics update immediately after every decision.

Tracked KPIs include:

* 💰 Cost Efficiency
* 🚚 Delivery Speed
* 🛡 Supply Chain Resilience
* 😊 Customer Satisfaction
* 🌱 Sustainability

Interactive progress bars provide instant visual feedback.

---

### 💡 Decision Feedback

After selecting an option, the simulator explains:

* Why the decision helps
* What risks it introduces
* Which KPIs improve
* Which KPIs decline

This reinforces business concepts through immediate cause-and-effect learning.

---

### 📊 Final Executive Dashboard

At the end of the simulation, users receive:

* Overall Supply Chain Score (0–100)
* Strength analysis
* Weakness analysis
* Biggest operational risk
* Three improvement recommendations
* Complete decision summary
* Final KPI dashboard

---

### 🔄 Replayability

Every replay generates:

* New company
* Different market conditions
* Different demand profile

This encourages exploration of multiple supply chain strategies.

# 📈 Scoring Logic

The overall Supply Chain Score is calculated using the average of five business metrics:

* Cost Efficiency
* Delivery Speed
* Resilience
* Customer Satisfaction
* Sustainability

Each strategic decision positively or negatively affects these metrics, emphasizing that no single strategy is optimal in every scenario.
