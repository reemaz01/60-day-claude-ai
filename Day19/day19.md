Day 19
Build a Football Intelligence Hub

Football Intelligence Hub

Overview

Football Intelligence Hub is an interactive football analysis experience that combines tournament forecasting, football knowledge assessment, and personality profiling. The system guides users through multiple stages to generate a personalized Football Intelligence Profile.

Features

1. FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Report

Analyzes historical tournament trends, team performance data, contender strength, and player information to generate:

* Most likely World Cup winner
* Predicted runner-up
* Dark horse nation
* Players to watch
* Confidence scores (0–100%)
* Supporting evidence and risk analysis

2. Football IQ Assessment

Interactive multiple-choice quiz that evaluates football knowledge across different difficulty levels.

Outputs:

* Football Awareness Score (0–100)
* Fan Classification:

  * Beginner Fan
  * Casual Viewer
  * Football Follower
  * Football Enthusiast
  * Football Expert
* Strength areas
* Knowledge gaps
* Personalized learning insights

3. Messi vs Ronaldo Personality Analysis

A personality-based assessment that compares user traits against characteristics commonly associated with Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo.

Evaluated Traits:

* Ambition
* Competitiveness
* Leadership
* Discipline
* Confidence
* Teamwork
* Creativity
* Work Ethic
* Learning Style
* Decision-Making Style

Outputs:

* Messi Compatibility Score (%)
* Ronaldo Compatibility Score (%)
* Trait-by-trait breakdown
* Personality explanation
* Behavioral insights

### 4. Football Personality Archetype

Based on assessment results, users are assigned one football personality archetype:

* Creative Playmaker
* Relentless Competitor
* Tactical Visionary
* Quiet Leader
* Fearless Attacker
* Strategic Commander
* Consistent Performer
* Big-Match Specialist

Each archetype includes:

* Description
* Core strengths
* Playing-style similarities
* Recommended football figures to study
