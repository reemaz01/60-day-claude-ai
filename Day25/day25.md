# 🦈 Shark Tank AI

Pitch your startup to AI-powered investors and receive realistic funding decisions, valuations, and strategic feedback.

![Shark Tank AI Banner](assets/banner.png)

## Overview

Shark Tank AI is an interactive startup evaluation simulator inspired by the Shark Tank investment process. Founders submit their startup idea, answer challenging investor questions, and receive AI-generated feedback, scores, valuations, and investment decisions.

The platform recreates a realistic venture capital pitch experience using multiple AI judges with unique investment perspectives.

---

## Features

### 🎤 Startup Pitch Submission

Users can provide:

* Startup Name
* Funding Ask
* Problem Statement
* Proposed Solution
* Revenue Model
* Target Audience

The platform automatically prepares the pitch for investor evaluation.

---

### 🦈 AI Investor Panel

Four AI judges analyze every startup from different viewpoints:

#### Victor Chen – Venture Capitalist

Evaluates:

* Market size
* Scalability
* Growth potential
* Unit economics
* Competitive moat

#### Maya Patel – Serial Founder

Evaluates:

* Product execution
* Founder capability
* Product-market fit
* Operational feasibility

#### Alex Kim – Customer Voice

Evaluates:

* Customer value
* User adoption
* Retention potential
* Market demand

#### Dana Ross – Angel Investor

Evaluates:

* Investment attractiveness
* Risk profile
* Profitability potential
* Funding readiness

---

### 💬 Interactive Pitch Round

The AI investors challenge founders with realistic questions such as:

* What is your competitive advantage?
* How will you acquire customers?
* What are your unit economics?
* Why will customers choose you over competitors?
* How scalable is your business model?

Founders respond in real time and the judges continuously reassess the startup.

---

### 📊 AI Startup Scoring Engine

Each pitch is scored across five key dimensions:

| Category              | Description                    |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Market Potential      | Size and growth of opportunity |
| Innovation            | Novelty and differentiation    |
| Business Model        | Revenue sustainability         |
| Execution             | Ability to deliver             |
| Investment Worthiness | Overall investor appeal        |

A final weighted score is generated out of 100.

---

### ⚖️ Investment Decisions

Based on performance, startups receive one of several outcomes:

* 💰 Invest
* 🤝 Acquire
* ⏰ Come Back Later
* ❌ Reject

Each decision includes detailed reasoning from the investor panel.

---

### 📝 Judge Feedback

Every investor provides:

* Individual verdict
* Investment rationale
* Concerns and risks
* Suggested improvements

This helps founders understand both strengths and weaknesses in their pitch.

---

### 💵 Valuation Analysis

The system generates:

* Suggested startup valuation
* Recommended funding amount
* Investment readiness assessment
* Growth milestone requirements

---

### 🏆 Leaderboard System

The platform stores pitch results locally and maintains a leaderboard featuring:

* Startup name
* Final score
* Valuation
* Verdict
* Date submitted

Users can compare multiple startup ideas over time.

## Example Startup Evaluation

### Startup

GreenLoop AI

### Problem

Electronic waste is growing rapidly while valuable materials are lost during disposal.

### Solution

An AI-powered recycling marketplace connecting users with certified recyclers while optimizing collection logistics.

### Result

* Overall Score: 63/100
* Market Potential: 72
* Innovation: 65
* Business Model: 61
* Execution: 58
* Investment Worthiness: 60

Verdict: **Come Back Later**

Reason:
Strong market opportunity and traction indicators, but investors require additional validation and scale before funding.

Shark Tank AI provides a safe environment where entrepreneurs can test ideas, receive feedback, and learn how investors evaluate startups before approaching real investors.
