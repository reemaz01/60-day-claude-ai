# AI Career Mentor – Business Strategy Report

## Overview

AI Career Mentor is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help Indian students (ages 13–25) make informed career decisions through personalized career reports, skill-gap analysis, learning roadmaps, internship recommendations, and interview preparation.

This repository contains the complete business strategy, market validation framework, revenue model, GTM strategy, competitive analysis, and execution roadmap for the startup.


## Problem Statement

Millions of students in India lack access to affordable and personalized career counseling.

### Key Challenges

* Traditional career counseling costs ₹5,000–₹50,000 per session.
* Limited access in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.
* Students rely on generic internet advice.
* Career decisions often lead to confusion and regret.



## Proposed Solution

AI Career Mentor provides:

* Personalized Career Reports
* Skill Gap Analysis
* Learning Roadmaps
* Internship Matching
* Interview Preparation
* Parent-Friendly Career Insights

### Core Value Proposition

Affordable AI-powered career guidance at a fraction of traditional counseling costs.


## Target Audience

### B2C

* Students (15–22 years)
* Class 10–12 students
* College students
* Parents seeking career guidance for children

### B2B

* Schools
* Colleges
* Career Guidance Centers
