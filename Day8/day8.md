Build AI-Powered Dashboard

Personal Environmental Health Analyzer
The Personal Environmental Health Analyzer is an interactive dashboard designed to assess environmental health risks using air quality and water quality indicators. The system analyzes AQI, PM2.5, PM10, TDS, and water hardness data to generate a personalized Environmental Health Score, health-risk assessment, insights, and actionable recommendations.

Key Features

Environmental Health Analysis

- Air Quality Index (AQI) monitoring
- PM2.5 and PM10 pollution analysis
- Water quality assessment (TDS & Hardness)
- Comparative city-level environmental benchmarking

Personal Report Card

- Environmental Health Score (0–100)
- Air Quality Score
- Water Quality Score
- Risk grading system (A–F)
- Hair and Skin Health Risk indicators

Insight Panel

- Top environmental risks identification
- Pollution trend analysis
- Cleanest and most polluted city comparisons
- Seasonal environmental observations
- Health impact interpretation

Personalized Recommendations

- Air pollution exposure reduction strategies
- Indoor air quality improvement suggestions
- Water quality management recommendations
- Skin and hair protection guidance
- Lifestyle and health optimization tips

Findings (Lucknow Case Study)

Environmental Status

- AQI: 152 (Poor)
- PM2.5: 78 µg/m³
- PM10: 143 µg/m³
- Water TDS: ~520 ppm
- Water Hardness: ~320 mg/L

Environmental Health Score

- Overall Score: 32/100
- Grade: D (Action Needed)

Key Risks

- High exposure to airborne particulate matter
- Increased respiratory health concerns
- Hard water-related hair and skin issues
- Reduced outdoor activity suitability during pollution peaks

Learning & Observations

Learnings

1. Air quality is the primary environmental health challenge.
2. PM2.5 contributes the greatest health risk.
3. Water quality significantly affects skin and hair health.
4. Environmental health requires analysis of multiple factors, not AQI alone.
5. Preventive actions can substantially reduce exposure risks.

Observations

- Lucknow experiences elevated pollution levels compared with cleaner Indian metros.
- Seasonal weather patterns strongly influence AQI.
- Water hardness remains a persistent quality concern.
- Small lifestyle changes can improve environmental health outcomes.
