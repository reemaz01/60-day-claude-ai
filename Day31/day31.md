# AI Supply Chain Control Tower

An interactive real-time supply chain simulation where players take on the role of **Head of Operations** responsible for keeping a global supply chain running during a high-pressure shift. The application presents live operational disruptions, requiring rapid decisions that directly impact business KPIs, revenue, and overall operational performance.

---

## Project Overview

The AI Supply Chain Control Tower recreates the experience of managing a modern enterprise supply chain during continuous disruptions. Players monitor operational metrics while responding to real-world scenarios including supplier delays, weather disruptions, customs inspections, warehouse shortages, machine failures, transportation breakdowns, and sudden demand spikes.

Each decision affects multiple business objectives simultaneously, forcing users to balance customer satisfaction, operational cost, inventory health, transport efficiency, and revenue protection.

## Features

* Real-time operational dashboard
* 3-minute timed simulation
* Dynamic disruption generation
* Multiple supply chain crisis scenarios
* Decision-based KPI updates
* Revenue protection tracking
* Live event log
* Progressive difficulty throughout the simulation
* Final performance grading (A+ to D)
* Pause and resume functionality
* Interactive help guide
* Responsive enterprise dashboard UI

---

## Business KPIs

The simulator continuously monitors:

* Service Level
* Customer Satisfaction
* Inventory Health
* Transport Efficiency
* Operating Cost Index
* Revenue Protected
* Operational Score

Every decision updates these KPIs in real time, demonstrating the interconnected nature of supply chain management.

---

##  Operational Scenarios

Players respond to disruptions such as:

* Port Congestion
* Supplier Delay
* Truck Breakdown
* Warehouse Stockout
* Customs Inspection
* Demand Spike
* Factory Machine Failure
* Weather Disruption
* Inventory Count Error
* Damaged Shipment

Each scenario includes multiple response strategies with different business consequences.

---

## Decision System

For every operational alert, users must choose among actions such as:

* Reroute Trucks
* Use Backup Supplier
* Transfer Inventory
* Increase Production
* Approve Air Freight
* Expedite Shipment
* Delay Decision
* Ignore Alert

Correct decisions improve operational performance, while poor decisions reduce KPIs and may introduce delayed consequences that impact future performance.

---

##  Final Score

Performance is evaluated using:

* Total Operational Score
* Revenue Protected
* KPIs at Shift Completion
* Alerts Successfully Resolved
* Correct vs Incorrect Decisions
* Final Leadership Grade (A+, A, B, C, D)

The grading system reflects both decision quality and operational consistency throughout the simulation.

---

##  Key Learnings

* Supply chain decisions involve balancing speed, cost, customer satisfaction, and operational resilience.
* Real-time decision-making is often more valuable than waiting for perfect information.
* Operational KPIs are interconnected; improving one metric may negatively affect another.
* Ignoring disruptions or delaying responses creates cascading downstream impacts.
* Backup suppliers, inventory redistribution, and transportation optimization are critical resilience strategies.
* Revenue protection depends on proactive operational planning rather than reactive crisis management.
* Enterprise dashboards help leaders prioritize high-impact disruptions using data-driven decision-making.
* Continuous monitoring enables organizations to minimize business risk during operational uncertainty.
