## 🧭 What Is This?

AI Career Mentor is an AI-powered platform that analyzes a student's interests,
skills, personality, and academic background to generate personalized career
recommendations, skill-gap analyses, learning roadmaps, and interview prep plans.

## ❗ The Problem We're Solving

580 million students in India make life-defining education and career decisions
with little to no quality guidance. Career counselling costs ₹5,000–₹50,000 per
session, is geographically concentrated in metros, and is often biased by
institutional commission arrangements.

The result: wrong stream choices, wasted years, and mismatched careers.

 ## 💡 Key Startup Insights

These are lessons learned during the validation phase — documented for future
co-founders, contributors, and investors.

### 1. The Market Gap Is Real and Specific
No competitor currently combines:
- Coverage of ages 13–25 in one continuous journey
- Sub-₹500 AI-first pricing
- Skill roadmap + learning path integration
- Interview preparation
- Zero institutional/commission bias

This is not a crowded space. It's a fragmented one with a clear white space.

### 2. Founder-Market Fit Is the Strongest Asset
Being a student *is* the unfair advantage. Every peer conversation is a
customer interview. Every personal frustration is a product requirement.
The challenge is converting empathy into structured product thinking.

### 3. The Enemy Is Not Competition — It's Premature Building
The biggest early risk is spending months building features before confirming
willingness-to-pay. A ₹99 pre-order page + 200 WhatsApp shares will teach
more than 3 months of coding.

### 4. B2C Validates, B2B Scales
Individual students prove the concept. Schools and colleges provide revenue
stability. The right sequencing: B2C traction first → use it as social proof
for B2B school contracts from Month 4 onwards.

### 5. AI Quality = Trust = Retention
In career guidance, a wrong recommendation isn't just a bad UX — it's a
life decision gone wrong. The AI layer must be treated as a safety-critical
system: confidence scoring, human review checkpoints, and clear disclaimers
are non-negotiable from Day 1.

### 6. Middle School Is the Unlocked Door
Every competitor starts at Class 10 or later. Starting at Class 8 (age 13)
creates a 4–7 year engagement funnel before competitors even enter the
student's life. Early trust compounds.

### 7. Parents Hold the Budget
Students feel the pain. Parents hold the wallet. Product design and pricing
must serve both — a "Parent View" dashboard and family plan pricing aren't
nice-to-haves, they're conversion levers.

## 🗺️ Roadmap

- [ ] 20 student discovery interviews
- [ ] 5 school counsellor interviews  
- [ ] Landing page with ₹99 pre-order CTA
